## Emberwild 0.9.1: no more kiting, everything scales forever

**Downloads:**
- `Emberwild-0.9.1-windows.zip` is the game.
- `Emberwild-0.9.1-windows-dev.zip` is the game plus the developer tools.

### The horde has to be fought
- **Smaller arenas:** each map is now about 128 blocks across (was about 220), with a narrower cliff band. Ruins, camps, shrines and altars are packed closer together.
- **A much bigger horde:** up to about 400 monsters at once by the last stage (was about 200). Packs come faster and larger, and "The horde closes in" and swarm events bring up to 90.
- **No running away:** when you run, many packs spawn ahead of you along your heading. From the Restless tier on, a wall of 12 to 30 monsters rises across your path every 20 to 30 seconds.
- Each monster has slightly less health to make up for the numbers. Area abilities shine.
- **Performance:** a spatial grid for crowd separation and area hits, and less animation and particle work for far-off monsters in a big horde.

### Everything scales forever
- **Weapon levels:** every weapon upgrade is a level, and **Hone** cards keep going without limit. +6% damage per level, longer melee reach, bigger fireballs, and every 4 levels another arrow, shard, skull or bolt, or 2 more revolver rounds. The HUD shows the weapon's level.
- **Raise Dead's level** also makes skeletons attack faster and last longer, and raises the army limit (24, plus one per two levels).
- **Chaos augments have no rank limit:** **Amplify** cards keep growing an augment past its written ranks. A run now holds 3 augments (was 2). Arcane Surge and Overload scale with the level of the ability you cast.
- Ability scaling audit: every ability's damage, including delayed hits, zones and follow-up blasts, now grows with its level.

### Dev build
- New dev menu entries: +5 weapon levels, +3 to every augment. "Spawn a horde" now spawns 60.

## Emberwild 0.9.0: three maps, two new classes, infinite abilities

**Downloads:**
- `Emberwild-0.9.0-windows.zip` is the game.
- `Emberwild-0.9.0-windows-dev.zip` is the game plus the developer tools.

### Runs
- **Three five-minute stages on three walled-in maps:** the Greenwood (Ancient Golem), then the Drowned Fen (Broodmother) or the Bone Wastes (Bone Colossus), picked on the title screen, then the Ember Throne (Ember King). A stage boss's death opens a portal to the next map; your character carries over.
- New terrain, blocks, textures, skies and monster rosters for the swamp, desert and volcanic maps.
- **Water and lava:** swim with a front crawl, tread water, climb out at the bank; lava burns and throws you back out.

### Classes
- **Necromancer:** Reaper's Scythe and Skull Staff; Raise Dead (allied skeletons), Bone Spear, Corpse Explosion, Soul Drain.
- **Gunslinger:** Twin Revolvers and a Lever Rifle; Fan the Hammer, Smoke Roll, Dynamite, Deadeye.

### Progression
- **Abilities stack without limit:** every card for an ability raises its level and every level grows it (Volley to a 360-degree ring, Smite to dozens of swords...). The first two abilities to reach level 10 become **Unbound** and keep growing; the others stop at 10.
- Bigger hordes whose health compounds across all three maps.
- **Meta progression is cosmetic only:** the Camp is a wardrobe of 38 outfits, headgear, capes, weapon finishes and auras. Old permanent upgrades are refunded as gold, and every class and relic is open.

### Look and feel
- **Animation:** turning in place (upper body first, then the feet step round), full cast animations for every ability, a nocking bow, gun recoil, a rifle lever and a revolver twirl reload, and swimming.
- **Interface:** a medieval voxel UI with stone panels, wooden plaque buttons that lift and glow on hover, parchment level-up cards, hanging class banners and stone ability slots.
- **Sound:** a mixer that stops spam (gaps, voice limits, repetition fatigue, pitch variation, ducking, salvos), softer re-voiced hits, and a distinct sound palette per class.
